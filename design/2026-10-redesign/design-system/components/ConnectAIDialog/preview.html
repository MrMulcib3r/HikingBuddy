Connect to AI

Let your AI app read and edit your HikingBuddy gear. Every change it makes shows up in Activity, where you can undo it.

  1. In Claude, open Customize › Connectors and click Add custom connector.
  2. Name it HikingBuddy, paste the server address below and click Add.
  3. Click Connect and sign in with the same Google account.
